Questions

Common questions

How much does a website or app cost?

The Basic Build is three 30-minute sessions: consult, deploy, and test. Bigger operations tools and AI agents get a fixed quote after the consult, before any work starts. Most sites cost almost nothing to run each year beyond your domain name.

Do I own my website?

Yes. Everything is set up on your own accounts, with your name and your domain. You get admin access and a simple guide, and there's no monthly lock-in.

Who updates the site after it's built?

Your own volunteers or staff. Group and event sites include a private admin page, so anyone on your team can post news or change the schedule from a phone.

Do you only work in Indianapolis?

We're based in Greenwood, Indiana, and meet in person across Indianapolis and Johnson County. Groups anywhere else work with us online.

What is an AI agent, and is it safe for a small group?

An AI agent handles busywork like answering member questions from your documents, drafting weekly emails, or sorting applications. It runs on your own accounts, and a person reviews anything that matters.

How fast can you build it?

A working version usually takes days, not months. Event sites can be ready well before your event.
